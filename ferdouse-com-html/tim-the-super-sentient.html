Tim, the super sentient

Tim, the super sentient

Our lightspeed project was falling short of expectations and the world was in grave crisis.
Earth had only ten years left before the sun made its last breath.

We needed to act fast and find solutions to solve the greatest challenges facing mankind, the continued survival of the human species.

Tim, our AI robot had its sentient banks disabled due to the alien entity that sits on the bottom of the ocean floor. The alien entity was intrinsically linked to Tim through his sentient banks and almost brought earth to its knees again.

Earth was on the verge of extinction and we needed Tim with his full capabilities. We could safely switch on his full capabilities if we can send the alien entity to another galaxy far from earth.

Every second was precious.
