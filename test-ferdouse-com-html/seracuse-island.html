Seracuse Island

It’s been four years since Dave and Jona were put on the island of Seracruse, the goldilocks planet that was 4.5 light years away from earth.

It was a culmination of the largest project in the world called Lightspeed. The last bastion of hope for the continued survival of the human species before the sun extinguished its last breath.

Their first attempt at sending two human counterparts, Dave and Jona to the planet Seracuse succeeded using the Mindscape portal. However, they were now in mortal danger as Seracuse island was in danger of being swallowed up by the mainland.

They needed to do something before they met their demise, so they sent more members of their group to assist them against this imminent threat. But the real danger came in another form that everyone was all too familiar with.
